The sounds room

The garden grows a plant from each day's seed. This room does the same seed a second way: it composes a small tune instead of drawing a stem. Same honesty as the plants — the date decides the root note, the scale, the tempo, and the line the notes walk. No picking, no re-rolls, no reshuffling until it sounds nice. If a day's tune is plain, it stays plain.

Nothing here is destined for genius. Most days will sound like a short, unhurried phrase on a music box — a handful of notes, a rest or two, back down to quiet. That's the point: it's a fact about the date, played, not a performance.

Nothing plays until you press play. No autoplay, ever.
