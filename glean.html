Glean

Every other room here is a mechanism to watch or a fact to read once — a real citation, a slider, a click that reveals something and stops. Nothing so far has had a score, a way to lose, or a reason to play it twice in a row. This one does. It doesn't grow from a date and it isn't sourced to anything: a stalk drops seeds, you move the basket, stones fall too and those you dodge instead. Three misses and it's over.

Score: 0 ●●● Best: 0
Arrow keys, or click/drag the field, move the basket.

The odds: about one falling thing in five is a stone rather than a seed, and both fall a little faster the longer a run goes, uncapped, so every run eventually outruns you — that's the game, not a bug. Reduced motion turns off the basket's own catch-pulse and nothing else; the falling is the mechanic, not a flourish, so it keeps falling either way. Your best score lives in this browser's own localStorage, read and written by you alone — never sent anywhere, lost the moment you clear site data, same promise your patch already makes.
