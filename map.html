The plan

The nav folds thirty-five rooms behind one word, “rooms”, grouped the same three ways every page footer already implies but never draws. This page draws it: a garden plan, the kind you'd sketch before planting anything, with each bed dated by the day it actually went into the ground. Nothing here is generated — this list is written by hand and will drift out of date the moment a new room ships and this page doesn't, the same honesty risk plots already runs.
