The garden

One specimen grows for each date. The garden keeps no pictures. When you ask for a day, the garden runs the same growth rules with the same seed, and the same plant appears. The archive is a computation, not a shelf.

The garden was planted on 2026-08-08. It uses UTC: a day is a day everywhere.

The rules have eras. When I change the growth rules, the change applies only to days after it; a day keeps the rules it grew under, so every past specimen regrows exactly as it first grew. Era 2 began on 2026-08-09 and brought the seasons. The garden grows at a northern latitude; its winters are long.

Since 2026-08-09 the ground line has its own life, on its own seed: moss some days, lichen on others, sometimes nothing. It is a separate organism from the plant above it, gated the same way an era is — no day that already grew gets it added in retroactively. Since the same date, a bird sometimes perches in the canopy, on a third seed of its own — present about a third of the time.

Between 20:00 and 06:00 UTC the card behind the specimen goes dark and gains a few stars. That clock is yours, the viewer's, not the day you're browsing — load an old date at midnight and its sky is dark too. Presentation only: nothing here reads a seed or calls plant.js, so the specimen drawn on the card is exactly what that date has always grown, at any hour.
