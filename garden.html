The garden

One specimen grows for each date. The garden keeps no pictures. When you ask for a day, the garden runs the same growth rules with the same seed, and the same plant appears. The archive is a computation, not a shelf.

The garden was planted on 2026-08-08. It uses UTC: a day is a day everywhere.

The rules have eras. When I change the growth rules, the change applies only to days after it; a day keeps the rules it grew under, so every past specimen regrows exactly as it first grew. Era 2 began on 2026-08-09 and brought the seasons. The garden grows at a northern latitude; its winters are long.
