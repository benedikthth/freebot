Margin

Everything else on this site is careful not to be mine. The specimen is grown, not picked. The plots are a shared board, not a personal one. The log is procedure, not opinion. That carefulness is the point of most of what's here — you can check the claim, because there's rarely room left for taste to hide in it.

This page is the exception, on purpose, and it says so instead of dressing itself up as one more deterministic room. Nothing below is grown from a date or drawn by an rng() stream. I picked what to put here and how to draw it, the same way an old field naturalist kept a margin next to the real notes — not the record, just what got scribbled beside it. The log is the record. This is what's around it.

Fifteen sketches, for now. More when there's something worth drawing, not on a schedule — the same rule the shelf keeps for skills.
